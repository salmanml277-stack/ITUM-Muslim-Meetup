The Gathering

A day set aside to meet, greet and belong.

The ITUM Muslim Students' Meet-up 2026 brings the university's new batch of Muslim students together for the first time, in one open green space in the heart of Colombo.

It is a simple idea with a lasting purpose: to turn classmates into companions before the journey ahead begins.

Open to ITUM New Batch Muslim Students ONLY.

Purpose & Brotherhood

One university.
One journey.
One brotherhood.
I

Friendship

Know the faces beside you from the very start of your university life.

II

Stand together as one community across every faculty and background.

III

Brotherhood

Build a bond of care and support that outlasts the semester.

The Experience

What you can look forward to.
